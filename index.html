Story Builder Privacy Policy

Story Builder · Effective 9 November 2026

Story Builder is an Android app for turning your own story text and pictures into an illustrated storybook. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Story Builder has no account, no server, and no cloud service.
  • Your stories and pictures stay on your phone. The app never uploads them.
  • We do not receive, sell, or share any personal or usage data.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking added by us.
  • The optional Pro purchase is handled by Google Play.

Purchases

Story Builder Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. To offer it, the app includes Google's Play Billing Library, which is why the app's permission list includes billing, internet, and network state. The library uses them to talk to the Play Store on your phone about what you own, and it may send Google technical information about purchases. Your payment details and purchase records are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or payment details. On the phone, the app remembers only whether Pro is unlocked.

Photos

When you add a picture to a page or cover, you choose it with Android's photo picker. The app receives only the photos you pick, never your whole gallery, and asks for no storage or camera permission. Each picked photo is copied into the app's private storage so the book keeps working if the original is moved or deleted.

What is stored on the phone

  • Your books: title, author name, page text, illustration ideas, layout choices, and the pictures you added.
  • Settings such as text size and where you last stopped reading or editing.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. If you have Android device backup turned on, Android may include it in your own backup under your Google account; we have no access to that backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything.

Export

You can export a book as a PDF, a set of PNG pages, or one long picture. Exported files are written only to a location you pick, and only when you ask. What you do with them afterwards (for example, sharing them) is up to you.

Children

Story Builder is intended for adults, such as parents and teachers making books. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
